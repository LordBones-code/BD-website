The Story

The Day the Sky Fell: While the Avengers repel a violent alien armada above New York City, Aiden Cross, an ordinary and dreamy teenager, discovers a mysterious cosmic necklace glowing with a magnetic blue light at the bottom of a crater.

As soon as he touches it, the artifact merges with his mind, granting him the power to materialize energy and manipulate gravity through the sheer force of his thoughts. Secretly supported by his family, Aiden learns to master his gifts under the name Levitation.

But the truce is short-lived: the object's creator, a ruthless alien scientist known as The Architect, lands on Earth determined to reshape New York's matter to reclaim his creation. Trapped in a destructive first battle, the young hero will have to rely on instinct and creativity to stand up against this geometric threat before Earth's mightiest heroes arrive.

Characters

Protagonist / Superhero

Aiden Cross (Levitation)

An ordinary, funny, and witty teenager whose life flips upside down when he finds the Core of Imagination in the form of a necklace. Guided by instinct, he fights with boundless creativity.

Powers: Free flight, levitation and movement of massive objects, materialization and projection of pure blue geometric energy shapes.

Antagonist / Creator

The Architect

An alien scientist and logician with dark skin laced with glowing circuitry. Calm and cold, he does not seek to conquer Earth, but only to regain control of his technological creation.

Powers: Absolute strategic intelligence, high-precision telekinesis, alteration and transformation of the structure of matter, creation of solid energy cages and structures.
